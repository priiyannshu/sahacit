## Item Notes

### 1. Indian and International Institutions
- Covers major Indian statutory/constitutional/executive institutions alongside key international organizations.
- Paired directly with **Missions and Programmes** (#2).

### 2. Missions and Programmes
- Focuses on missions, initiatives, and programmes launched by the national and international institutions covered in #1.

### 3. Committees
- High-yield static reference of major commissions and committees and what they are known for.

### 4. Subject Dictionaries
- Multiple subject-specific dictionaries (aligned with the 7 core categories where needed) aimed at making subject vocabulary effortless and fluent when reading tricky UPSC Prelims statements.

### 5. Dictionary of Ancient Texts
- Dedicated static reference for ancient texts—building fast recall in Ancient History and Art & Culture.

### 6. Reforms
- Static compilation of major reforms across domains for fast comparative recall.

### 7. Economic Policies
- Static reference of major economic policies, frameworks, and terminology.
