## Core Concept

Build an **AI Examiner Board**—a team of examiner agents powered by custom **reusable skills** grounded in empirical research on **UPSC Prelims question patterns and question types**. Rather than generic quizzes, the agents test material in the exact structural ways UPSC Prelims frames and traps candidates.

## Daily Ingestion-to-Testing Loop (2027)

1. **Daily Diary Tracking**: Each day's structured `entry.md` records all information ingested that day.
2. **Digital-Mirror Tracking**: Track physical printed materials alongside their **digital copies** (under `booklets/`) so the system knows precisely what facts and themes have been read each day.
3. **Pattern-Matched Mock & Question Generation**: Examiner agents pull from the day's ingested content (plus prior entries) and apply pattern-research skills to continuously generate questions and mock tests.
4. **Interval-Based Spaced Repetition**: Re-surface and re-test previously ingested facts at optimal intervals for maximum long-term retention, always framed in authentic UPSC Prelims question styles.
