Observed from the **2026 GS Paper 1** breakdown and a prior **1-session research video**:

- **Focus Distribution Within History**:

  - **Modern History**: Heavily tested / major focus.
  - **Ancient History & Art and Culture**: Strong focus on areas like **Harappan civilization**, **coins**, and **Vedas**, closely paired with **Art & Culture** topics such as **paintings** and **caves**.
