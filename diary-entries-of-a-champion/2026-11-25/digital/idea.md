# Digital Tools & Cognitive Aids — Idea

## Core Thesis

Combine **AI preparation**, **physical printing (digital-to-reality)**, and **software/AI development know-how** to build custom cognitive aids for learning—creating an asymmetric advantage over standard exam preparation workflows.

## 1. Study Time Recorder PWA & Database

- **Purpose**: Operate with disciplined consistency over the 6-month runway to **23 May 2027 Prelims** by tracking every hour and minute spent studying (accounting for minimal final-year college work while owning virtually all remaining hours of the day).
- **Capabilities**:
  - Record study sessions down to exact **hours and minutes**.
  - Persist all logged time data into a **structured database** for downstream analytics and integrations.
  - Support **weekly and monthly evaluations** so progress and time allocation stay measurable against preparation targets.

## 2. Digital-to-Reality Print Generators

- Custom software/AI pipelines to generate structured, print-ready artifacts directly feeding the physical study directories:
  - **Books** (`../books/`)
  - **Booklets** (`../booklets/`)
  - **Character Sheets** (`../character-sheets/`)
  - **Timeline Cutouts & Information Packets** (`../timeline-badges-and-cutouts/`)
