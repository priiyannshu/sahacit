# Character Sheets — Idea

## Core Concept: Characters Before Storylines

- View the entire UPSC syllabus as a **connected narrative** populated by prominent **characters** who each tie together large clusters of facts and themes.
- **Learning Sequence**:
  1. **First**, build deep familiarity with the **characters** themselves.
  2. **Later**, step into the **storylines** (events, movements, policies, eras) that connect those characters.

## Physical Sheet Format

- **Visual Anchor**: Include a **picture** of each character wherever possible—either a **real image** or **AI-generated** guided by explicit visual input.
- **High-Density Information**: Attach comprehensive connected information around each character.
- **Duplex & Multi-Page Expansion**:
  - Print **front and back** on physical sheets.
  - Extend onto **additional pages** whenever a character carries enough information to require more space.
