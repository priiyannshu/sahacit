## Concepts (in narrative order)

### Rock Families

1. **igneous-rock** — Igneous Rock
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_igneous_rock
   What: Rocks formed by cooling and solidification of magma or lava; intrusive types (granite) cool slowly underground, coarse-grained; extrusive types (basalt) cool fast at surface, fine-grained.

2. **granite** — Granite
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_granite
   What: Coarse-grained intrusive igneous rock composed of quartz, feldspar, and mica; hard, light-coloured; forms continental interiors and ancient shields.

3. **basalt** — Basalt
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_basalt
   What: Fine-grained extrusive igneous rock; dark, dense; poured out at surface; Deccan Plateau is basalt at continental scale — 66 Ma lava outpour, now parent of fertile black soils.

4. **sedimentary-rock** — Sedimentary Rock
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_sedimentary_rock
   What: Formed by weathering, transport, deposition, and lithification of sediments; layered; fossil-bearing; hosts coal, oil, and natural gas in sedimentary basins.

5. **sandstone** — Sandstone
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_sandstone
   What: Mechanically formed sedimentary rock of cemented sand grains (mainly quartz); common building stone.

6. **limestone** — Limestone
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_limestone
   What: Organically formed sedimentary rock of calcium carbonate (CaCO₃); raw material for cement and steelmaking flux; wide domestic distribution lowers transport costs.

7. **metamorphic-rock** — Metamorphic Rock
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_metamorphic_rock
   What: Existing rocks transformed by heat and pressure without fully melting; marble from limestone, slate from shale, schist and gneiss from igneous rocks under mountain-building pressure.

8. **marble** — Marble
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_marble
   What: Non-foliated metamorphic rock formed from limestone under heat and pressure; used in construction and sculpture.

9. **slate** — Slate
   Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_slate
   What: Fine-grained foliated metamorphic rock from low-grade metamorphism of shale; splits into thin layers; used as roofing material.

10. **rock-cycle** — Rock Cycle
    Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_rock_cycle
    What: Continuous process linking all three rock families — igneous weathers to sediment, which lithifies and may metamorphose, and eventually melts back to magma.

### Minerals and Ores

11. **mineral** — Mineral
    Source: ncert — 11th_p1_ch05_minerals_and_rocks:geo_mineral
    What: Naturally occurring inorganic substance with a definite chemical composition and characteristic physical properties; over 3,000 known; only a handful form common rocks.

12. **ore-formation-processes** — Ore Formation Processes
    Source: writer-ai (pending: leong)
    What: Geological processes concentrating minerals into economically viable deposits — hydrothermal deposition in fractures, sedimentary accumulation, weathering-residual concentration, placer deposits on coastlines.
    enhancement_target: true

13. **mineral-types** — Mineral Classification
    Source: ncert — 8th_ch03_mineral_and_power_resources:geo_mineral_types
    What: Three working categories — metallic (ferrous: iron, manganese, chromite; non-ferrous: copper, gold, bauxite, aluminium); non-metallic (limestone, mica, gypsum, salt); energy minerals (coal, petroleum, uranium, thorium).

### India's Four Mineral Belts

16. **mineral-belts** — India's Four Mineral Belts
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_mineral_belts
    What: NE Plateau (Chotanagpur — Gondwana shield), SW Plateau (Karnataka/Goa — Dharwar system), NW Belt (Aravalli — ancient non-ferrous), Himalayan Belt (under-explored, young erosion); each belt reflects distinct geological history.

17. **northeastern-plateau-belt** — North-Eastern Plateau Belt (Chotanagpur)
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_chota_nagpur
    What: Precambrian shield across Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal; densest mineral zone in India — coal, iron ore, manganese, mica, bauxite, chromite, copper concentrated by 2 billion years of geological time.

18. **singhbhum** — Singhbhum (Iron Ore and Copper)
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)
    What: Jharkhand district; primary location for iron ore and copper within the NE Plateau Belt; sits on Precambrian Dharwar-type basement rocks.
    enhancement_target: true

19. **hazaribagh-kodarma-mica** — Hazaribagh-Kodarma Mica Belt
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_mica
    What: Districts in Jharkhand; produce India's highest-quality muscovite mica used in electrical and electronics insulation; India was world's leading mica exporter through the 20th century.

20. **southwestern-plateau-belt** — South-Western Plateau Belt (Karnataka/Goa)
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_mineral_belts
    What: Iron ore at Kudremukh and Bellary-Hospet (Karnataka), gold at Kolar (closed 2001) and Hutti; Goa iron ore exported through Mormugao port to Japan and South Korea.

21. **kolar-gold-mine** — Kolar Gold Mine
    Source: writer-ai
    What: One of world's deepest gold mines, Karnataka; produced ore for over a century; closed 2001 after exhaustion; historically India's largest gold source.

22. **northwestern-belt** — North-Western Belt (Aravalli)
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)
    What: Aravalli Range through Rajasthan into Gujarat; among Earth's oldest fold mountains; non-ferrous metals from ancient hydrothermal processes — copper at Khetri, lead-zinc at Zawar and Rampura-Agucha.
    enhancement_target: true

23. **khetri-copper** — Khetri Copper Mine
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)
    What: Rajasthan; one of India's largest copper-mining complexes, in the Aravalli belt.
    enhancement_target: true

24. **zawar-rampura-agucha** — Zawar and Rampura-Agucha Lead-Zinc Mines
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)
    What: Rajasthan; Zawar is one of world's oldest continuously worked mines (ancient smelting evidence); Rampura-Agucha among world's largest zinc-lead deposits.
    enhancement_target: true

25. **himalayan-mineral-belt** — Himalayan Mineral Belt
    Source: writer-ai
    What: Under-explored — copper, lead, zinc, cobalt, tungsten identified in scattered pockets; young Himalayan erosion has not yet exposed deeper deposits; strategic future unwritten.

### Key Minerals — Iron Ore

26. **india-iron-ore** — India's Iron Ore
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_iron_ore
    What: India holds some of world's largest haematite reserves; among top global producers — Singhbhum (Jharkhand), Bailadila (Chhattisgarh), Kudremukh (Karnataka), Keonjhar-Sundergarh (Odisha leads production).

27. **bailadila** — Bailadila Iron Ore Complex
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_iron_ore
    What: Fourteen-deposit complex in Bastar hills, Chhattisgarh; much exported to Japan and South Korea under trade arrangements dating to the 1960s.

28. **haematite-magnetite** — Haematite and Magnetite Grades
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)
    What: Haematite — high iron content, found across Gondwana-shield zones (Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh); magnetite — harder to process, found in Karnataka.
    enhancement_target: true

### Key Minerals — Coal

29. **india-coal** — India's Coal
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_coal
    What: India among world's three largest coal producers; 98% is Gondwana coal (Permian, ~250 Ma); predominantly non-coking bituminous; fuels ~55% of electricity generation.

30. **damodar-valley-coalfields** — Damodar Valley Coalfields
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_coal_india
    What: India's largest coalfield cluster — Raniganj (West Bengal), Jharia (Jharkhand, largest by reserve, underground fires burning over a century), Bokaro, Giridih.

31. **gondwana-coal** — Gondwana Coal
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_coal_india
    What: Permian-age coal (~250 Ma) from buried Gondwana forests; 98% of India's reserves; in Damodar Valley and Mahanadi, Godavari, Wardha basins.

32. **tertiary-coal** — Tertiary Coal (Northeast India)
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_coal_india
    What: Younger coal (15–20 Ma) in Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh; lower carbon content than Gondwana coal.

33. **coking-coal-deficit** — India's Coking Coal Deficit
    Source: writer-ai
    What: India imports millions of tonnes of coking coal annually; domestic coal is overwhelmingly non-coking bituminous; steelmaking requires coking coal that India's geology does not supply adequately.

### Key Minerals — Mica, Bauxite, Manganese

34. **india-mica** — Mica in India
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_mica
    What: India was world's leading mica exporter; Hazaribagh-Kodarma (Jharkhand) produces highest-quality muscovite; also Nellore (Andhra Pradesh) and Jaipur-Bhilwara-Udaipur (Rajasthan).

35. **bauxite-india** — Bauxite in India
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_bauxite
    What: Ore of aluminium found in laterite rocks; Odisha leads (Kalahandi, Sambalpur); also Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, Gujarat.

36. **manganese-india** — Manganese in India
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_manganese
    What: Used in steel alloying and battery technology; mainly from Odisha, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh.

40. **sambhar-lake** — Sambhar Lake
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)

### Coastal Strategic Minerals and Thorium

41. **kerala-tn-beach-sands** — Kerala-Tamil Nadu Coastal Heavy Mineral Suite
    Source: writer-ai (pending: majid-india)
    What: Beach sands of Kerala-TN coast contain ilmenite and rutile (titanium sources), zircon, sillimanite, and monazite; concentrated by marine processes over geological time; a strategic resource.
    enhancement_target: true

42. **monazite** — Monazite
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_nuclear_energy
    What: Mineral in Kerala-TN beach sands containing thorium and rare-earth elements; prescribed substance — only government bodies can process or export it.

43. **thorium-nuclear-strategy** — India's Thorium-Based Nuclear Strategy
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_nuclear_energy
    What: India's three-stage nuclear programme designed to eventually use thorium as fuel; monazite sands hold one of world's largest known thorium reserves; current reactor technology cannot yet use it.

### Petroleum and Natural Gas

44. **india-petroleum** — India's Petroleum
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_petroleum
    What: India imports ~85% of crude oil; geology is coal-rich and oil-poor; major fields — Digboi (Assam, 1889), Mumbai High (largest), KG basin, Cambay (Gujarat), Barmer (Rajasthan).

45. **digboi** — Digboi Oilfield
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_petroleum_india
    What: Assam; discovered 1889; one of world's oldest continuously worked oilfields; India's first oil discovery.

46. **mumbai-high** — Mumbai High Offshore Field
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_petroleum
    What: India's largest producing oilfield in Arabian Sea; discovered 1974; accounted for ~40% of domestic crude at peak; reserves declining.

47. **krishna-godavari-basin** — Krishna-Godavari Basin
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_natural_gas
    What: Major natural gas province offshore Andhra Pradesh (Reliance D6 block); emerged 2000s as significant gas source; part of India's eastern coastal energy zone.

48. **natural-gas-india** — Natural Gas in India
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_india_natural_gas
    What: Same geography as petroleum — Mumbai High, KG basin, Cambay, Tripura, Rajasthan; GAIL built national pipeline network to move gas from producing areas to industrial consumers.

### Non-Conventional Energy

49. **solar-energy-india** — Solar Energy in India
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_solar_potential
    What: ~300 clear sunny days annually, especially in Rajasthan and Gujarat; major parks — Bhadla (Rajasthan, Thar Desert), Pavagada (Karnataka), Kurnool (Andhra Pradesh).

50. **international-solar-alliance** — International Solar Alliance
    Source: writer-ai
    What: India co-founded; headquartered at Gurugram; groups tropical countries to accelerate solar deployment; energy diplomacy rooted in India's high-insolation geography.

51. **wind-energy-india** — Wind Energy in India
    Source: ncert — 10th_ch05_minerals_and_energy_resources:geo_wind_power_india
    What: Strongest along southern peninsula coasts and Western Ghats passes; major farms — Muppandal (Tamil Nadu, largest in Asia), Jaisalmer, Kutch coast; 5th-largest installed wind capacity globally.

52. **nuclear-energy-india** — Nuclear Energy in India
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_nuclear_energy
    What: Uranium at Jaduguda (Jharkhand, main mine) and Tummalapalle (Andhra Pradesh); limited uranium reserves; three-stage programme makes thorium the long-term fuel base.

53. **jaduguda** — Jaduguda Uranium Mine
    Source: writer-ai
    What: Main operational uranium mine in India, Singhbhum district, Jharkhand; only major uranium mining complex currently in operation.

54. **geothermal-india** — Geothermal Energy in India
    Source: ncert — 8th_ch03_mineral_and_power_resources:geo_geothermal_energy
    What: Sites at Manikaran (Himachal Pradesh) and Puga Valley (Ladakh); not commercially significant yet.

55. **tidal-energy-india** — Tidal Energy in India
    Source: ncert — 12th_p1_ch07_mineral_and_energy_resources:geo_non_conventional_energy
    What: Theoretically feasible in Gulf of Kutch and Gulf of Khambhat (large tidal ranges); no commercial plant exists.

### Structural Overlap: Adivasi Lands and Mining

56. **adivasi-mineral-overlap** — Adivasi-Mineral Structural Overlap
    Source: writer-ai
    What: India's Gondwana coal and Chotanagpur minerals are concentrated in Scheduled V tribal homelands — thin soils on ancient shield rock left land as forest; mining directly conflicts with Adivasi customary forest and land rights.

57. **pesa-act-1996** — PESA Act 1996
    Source: writer-ai
    What: Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act; extends gram sabha governance to Schedule V tribal areas; requires gram sabha consent for land acquisition in tribal regions.

58. **forest-rights-act-2006** — Forest Rights Act 2006
    Source: writer-ai
    What: Recognises forest rights of Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers on land they have historically occupied; intended to remedy colonial-era dispossession of forest access.
